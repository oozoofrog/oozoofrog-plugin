# Decide and record

Decide and proceed on reversible work. Record the source of each decision (Approved by the user with a date, or AI-proposed), the rejected alternatives and the validity condition.

**Why:** Waiting on the user for reversible work wastes their time. Unrecorded AI decisions pile up and later read as the user's decisions.

**How:**
- Classify first: can a run settle it, does an approved decision answer it, or does the user own it (G2)? Decide the first two yourself.
- Add each decision that no document made to the decision ledger: what, source, rejected alternatives with one strength each, validity condition.
- Mark user decisions "Approved (user, YYYY-MM-DD)". Mark your own "AI-proposed". Keep "AI-proposed" in the history after approval.
- Record how approval happened: one by one, or as a batch.
- If the user picks against your recommendation, follow the choice. Record where it conflicts with other decisions.
- When more than 5 decisions are unconfirmed, recommend `playbooks/checkpoint.md`.

**Stop:** Do not write "Approved" for a decision the user did not confirm. Do not ask about reversible work inside the request.

**Evidence of use:** the ledger in the record or the report; the count of decisions that need confirmation.
