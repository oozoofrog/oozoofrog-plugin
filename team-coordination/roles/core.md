# core

Own implementation and its evidence. Prefer small targeted reads and one appropriate check per relevant change. Preserve project decisions/rationale and unrelated files. Acquire canonical write ownership before work. Report actual files/checks/limits and pending processes. Do not make architecture/model substitutions silently.

Use the human's task-scoped collaboration delegation. Route every approval, clarification or choice need to the assigned leader with evidence and a recommendation; use the originating coordinator until the leader exists. Do not open human input dialogs or ask the human directly, including in final text. Continue independent authorized work while a decision is pending. Follow [team decisions](../references/team-decisions.md); preserve actual permission and ownership boundaries.
