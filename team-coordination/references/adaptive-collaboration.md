# Adapt collaboration from task evidence

Use this guidance during authorized team formation and execution. Improve delivery while learning which session conditions and collaboration methods fit the work. This is a decision procedure, not an automatic model router, telemetry service or permission to rewrite the skill. Explanations and skill maintenance create no team.

## Initial allocation is a testable hypothesis

The current coordinator or authorized formation owner records the smallest useful arrangement, including direct work when sufficient, the reason for each assignment and the uncertainty that could change it. Start with current human choices and requested defaults under [profiles](profiles.md); these are constraints and starting points, not measured capability rankings. Keep model, reasoning effort, service mode, specialty and authority distinct. Do not add a role solely to fill a model tier.

Describe the work before choosing a session:

- Required input and tools: code, documents, screens or cross-application actions.
- Ambiguity, cross-file dependencies and decisions that must remain consistent.
- Observable acceptance criteria, hidden-error risk and available independent checks.
- Recovery cost, reversibility and the user's time/cost constraints.

Mark the basis as official guidance, analogous task experience, current-project evidence or untested. Do not invent suitability scores, success probabilities or cost savings. A model name or generic benchmark alone does not establish suitability for this task. Give each assignment an observable completion condition and a reason to revisit it; do not schedule constant reassessment.

## Observe the session, then diagnose

Executors report material deviations through normal task evidence: what was requested, what happened, artifact/check references, missing inputs and the next useful action. Retain representative successes as well as failures. An explicitly assigned dedicated leader coordinates this assessment without taking over investigation, implementation or testing; direct work in the originating chat does not require this role split.

Judge the session under its actual conditions: model/effort observations where available, supplied context, scope, tools, environment, instructions and review method. Separate observations from causal interpretations. A configuration receipt does not attest provider execution identity. Unknown conditions remain unknown.

Before attributing failure to the model, distinguish missing context, ambiguous instructions, excessive scope, tool/environment/access failure and reasoning or instruction-following failure. Use [doctor](doctor.md) for supported collaboration diagnostics. Capacity or access failure is not evidence of low task ability.

The leader chooses the smallest useful intervention within existing authority: clarify acceptance, supply context, split work, change the checking method, or consider another session/model/effort. Change one relevant factor when practical. If urgency requires several changes, record the confounding factors and avoid claiming which change caused improvement.

Model changes must respect explicit human selections, exact supported controls and [session control](session-control.md). Do not silently substitute, expand permissions, interrupt an uncertain producer or duplicate a write owner. Routine adjustments within delegated scope need no new approval ceremony; fixed human constraints remain binding. A handoff preserves work, failures, ownership and required acknowledgements at a supported safe boundary.

## Review the allocation decision independently

The leader owns immediate adjustments and delivery. An independent reviewer checks consequential allocation claims and proposed reusable lessons against original requirements, assigned instructions, current artifacts and verification evidence, not just the leader's account. Where practical, inspect raw evidence before the leader's causal conclusion.

Reuse the existing reviewer for small tasks. First use existing eligible review capacity. Consider a separate, bounded reviewer session only with explicit creation authority and a concrete unmet independence need whose value outweighs the extra work. Repeated failures or frequent handoffs alone are reasons to diagnose or simplify collaboration, not to add another reviewer. Creation still requires existing team-creation authority and supported controls. This is an assignment of the reviewer role, not a new mandatory policy role or an always-on audit team. A reviewer must not independently approve an allocation decision or intervention they designed, authored or co-authored, even when the leader formally approved it. Rechecking a corrected product is allowed; auditing the cause of that reviewer's own earlier miss or the benefit of their proposed remedy requires another eligible reviewer. If none is available, retain the learning claim as provisional or insufficient evidence without waiving required independent product review.

Reversible task adjustments may proceed within existing authority without waiting for a learning audit. Consequential claims include attributing failures to a model, asserting a benefit that will guide later allocation, or proposing a reusable operating rule. Independently review those claims before representing them as established or adopting the rule; existing handoff and product-review gates still apply.

Check whether the intervention addressed the evidenced cause, whether omitted work or weaker checks explain speed gains, and whether verification or rework displaced effort elsewhere. Keep delivery acceptance separate from the learning verdict: supported under stated conditions, provisional, rejected or insufficient evidence. An inconclusive allocation claim need not block otherwise verified delivery; it must not become a proven rule. A supported lesson does not make a failing deliverable acceptable.

## Measure accepted outcomes and keep a compact record

Compare elapsed time to an accepted result, requirement omissions, regressions, retries, review/rework effort and human intervention where observed. Record provider usage/cost only when supplied; missing telemetry stays unavailable. Do not infer billing from elapsed time or create internal token budgets. Preserve acceptance criteria, failures, required checks and difficult cases; lowering the bar or avoiding required work is not an improvement.

For a material decision, link a compact entry from the existing authoritative project task record using its supported evidence/decision mechanism:

| Field | Content |
| --- | --- |
| Task and conditions | Acceptance criteria, task class, relevant model/effort/context/tool observations and unknowns |
| Observation | Expected versus observed result, with current artifact/check references |
| Interpretation | Suspected cause, competing explanation and strength of evidence |
| Intervention | What changed, owner, reason and applicable scope/authority |
| Outcome | Accepted-result evidence, observed time/rework/usage and remaining uncertainty |
| Review and reuse | Independent judgment, applicability, invalidation conditions and rollback/revisit condition |

This is a content template, not new managed-state fields or a second ledger. Use existing task evidence/notes and reference them in the current packet. Do not directly edit managed state, invent an event/API or mutate global memory. If the current record cannot store an entry, retain it in the assigned task report and identify that persistence limit. Never label self-review as independent review.

## Apply lessons at the right scope

- **Current task:** Apply justified reversible adjustments within the goal, ownership and human constraints. Record before/after evidence; revert ineffective changes when safe. Reuse an accepted method until relevant conditions or new failures change.
- **Project:** Treat repeated, independently reviewed observations as a conditional operating rule. Preserve the previous rule, replacement rationale and counterexamples. Carry adopted guidance in current packets with required ACKs; managed contract changes use their supported safe-adoption path. One successful trial remains provisional.
- **Shared skill or defaults:** Propose a separate, scoped change with applicable task evidence, regression cases and limits. Apply only within an authorized skill/default-maintenance task and validate affected distribution surfaces. Team operation alone grants no authority to rewrite shared skills, user model choices or global configuration. Source changes, installation, discovery and active-team adoption remain separate.

Compare new candidates on representative, reversible tasks within the existing goal and constraints. Use comparable inputs and acceptance criteria, disclose differing tool/context/effort conditions, and repeat only when variability or contradictory evidence warrants it. Never run competing writers or repeat external side effects for an experiment. Prefer completed artifacts or isolated fixtures when suitable; label simulated evidence accordingly. No standing benchmark program, extra team or fixed trial/review quota is implied.

Revisit a lesson when the model/version, instructions, context construction, tools, task class or acceptance criteria materially change; retain old evidence with its conditions. Stop optional experimentation when it yields no decision-relevant evidence or delays the deliverable. Continue required diagnosis and verification. Learning is useful only when it improves authorized work.

For example: a reviewer missed a caller compatibility issue when given only a changed file, then found it after caller context was supplied. This supports changing review inputs under those conditions. It does not establish general model unsuitability or that another model would have performed better.
